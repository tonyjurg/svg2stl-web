One SVG · two useful outcomes

Form or stencil.
You choose the material.

A

SOLID FORM

The path becomes the object.

Every closed contour is extruded as material—ideal for badges, tokens, ornaments, signs, and flat relief components.

  • No surrounding plate
  • Original proportions preserved
  • Several contours can become several solids
2D paths solid_shape.stl
B

STENCIL PLATE

The path becomes the opening.

Closed contours are removed from a bordered plate—ready for paint masks, craft templates, labels, and repeatable markings.

  • Configurable outer border
  • Clean through-openings
  • One validated printable plate
closed paths stencil.stl

Confidence before download

A file is not a result.
A checked mesh is.

The service rejects output that fails its printability contract. Every successful download includes measurements and topology signals you can inspect before opening your slicer.
